Privacy Policy — Simple OBDII

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Simple OBDII is a diagnostic tool that connects to your vehicle through a Bluetooth OBD-II (ELM327) adapter. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information the app uses

  • Bluetooth. The app connects only to a Bluetooth adapter that you pair and select. Bluetooth is used solely to exchange diagnostic data with your vehicle.
  • Vehicle data. The app reads diagnostic information from your vehicle — such as RPM, speed, temperatures, trouble codes, and the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) — and displays it on your device. This data is not stored by the app after you close it, and it is never sold.

Information sent over the internet

The app's only internet use is the recall check. When you connect, the app sends your vehicle's VIN to the United States National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) — specifically its vPIC and Recalls APIs (api.nhtsa.gov) — to look up the vehicle and any recall campaigns for its model year. The VIN goes only to NHTSA, a U.S. government service. The app has no server of its own, contains no advertising, and includes no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Data storage and sharing

  • The app does not create an account and does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or photos.
  • No personal data is collected, stored, or shared with third parties other than the VIN-to-NHTSA recall lookup described above.

Children

The app is a vehicle tool and is not directed at children.
